SO100 Cotrain vs SO100-only Counterfactual Deep Dive

Same 165 near-apricot query frames. FM seeds 0/1/2 are averaged per frame. Deltas are Cotrain minus SO100-only.

Key Findings

Not uniformly worse at every horizon.
In approach 50-70px, Cotrain has positive early progress vs SO100 60k through step 10, and a smaller early advantage vs SO100 80k at step 1.
But final approach is worse in every phase.
At horizon step 30, Cotrain final progress delta is negative for close, near, and approach against both SO100-only baselines.
Main decomposition.
By step 30, Cotrain usually has lower target-axis progress and higher gripper value; in close phase it also carries more lateral offset.
