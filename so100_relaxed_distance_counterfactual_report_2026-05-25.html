SO100 Relaxed Distance Counterfactual

Threshold relaxed to 150px across the same two rollouts: 180 query frames. New samples beyond 70px are sparse: 70-95 has 8 frames, 95-150 has 7 frames.

Key Finding

Direction advantage does get stronger farther out.
For 70-150px, Cotrain first10 direction-cos delta is positive vs both SO100-only baselines, with 60-73% frame win rate.
But it is not enough evidence for a firm claim.
The widened region adds only 15 frames, so this is a signal to test on more rollouts rather than a stable statistical conclusion.
Approach still differs from execution.
Even with better early direction, Cotrain can lose final progress by step 30 because target-axis progress is not sustained.
